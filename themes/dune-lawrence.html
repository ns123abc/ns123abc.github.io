Sample march

Sample. 2 July. We left the second ridge while the ground was still cold and marked the bend in the watercourse. Bearings went in the margin. The rest of the page waited until the paper would take ink.

Sources Sand and the stillsuit band follow Denis Villeneuve’s Dune (2021), photographed by Greig Fraser, with costumes, including the stillsuits, by Jacqueline West and Bob Morgan. The header follows the film’s title treatment, custom letterforms that read the same turned upside down, set here in a widely tracked system face. The diary page and the map plate follow T. E. Lawrence, Seven Pillars of Wisdom: a Triumph (subscribers’ edition, 1926). Manning Pike printed the text with the assistance of H. J. Hodgson, and John Bartholomew & Son drew the folded maps.
