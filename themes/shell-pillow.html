Things that almost hold

  • Spring Water on the step: it still belongs to the night, and the first person through it will believe they were first.
  • Autumn A persimmon sleeve: the only warm thing among the shutters, and aware of it.
  • Winter Breath on the window: the street is written in it, and then the street takes the writing back.

A list is how a court keeps a thing it will not explain. Three is enough. A fourth would be greed, or a diary, and I am not in the mood to confess. The water, the sleeve, and the glass can stand in the doorway a moment longer.
