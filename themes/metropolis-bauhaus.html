sources

  • Fritz Lang, Metropolis, Universum-Film. Premiere 10 January 1927, Ufa-Palast am Zoo, Berlin. Sets by Otto Hunte, Erich Kettelhut, and Karl Vollbrecht. The Friedrich-Wilhelm-Murnau-Stiftung restoration premiered in February 2010 from the 16mm duplicate negative found in Buenos Aires in 2008. Project notes.
  • Art of the Title credits Erich Kettelhut with the title cards. The black card and the capitals here follow that idea. They do not redraw a card. Metropolis (1927).
  • Heinz Schulz-Neudamm, poster for Metropolis, 1926, printed by Paul Grasnick, Berlin, for UFA. Museum of Modern Art, 80.1961.1. The yellow street is this specimen’s black-and-sulfur note. It does not use the poster image. MoMA.
  • Walter Gropius, Bauhaus building, Dessau. Opening ceremony 4 December 1926. The page grid is that curtain-wall module. Bauhaus-Archiv chronology.
  • From 1922, workshop products that received approval were signed with a stamp. Same chronology. The plate above is marked SAMPLE. It is not that stamp.
  • Herbert Bayer, Universal, 1925. A single-case alphabet drawn with circle and straightedge, while he led the Dessau printing and advertising workshop. The lowercase lines follow that rule. Letterform Archive.
  • Circle, square, and triangle: the elementary forms of the preliminary course begun by Johannes Itten. Getty Research Institute, Primary Forms.
  • In 1923 Gropius named the new direction as art and technology, a new unity. Same Bauhaus-Archiv chronology. The rings and the red rule are the machine, drawn as geometry, not taken from a frame.
