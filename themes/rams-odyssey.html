SAMPLE · Lamp steady. No fault.

SAMPLE. The ground is the stark white of the ship and the last room. The black band is the title card: a widely spaced sans, centered. The lamp is the only color. It is warm at the core and red at the rim, and it reports a state. The labels under the band are a control row. A mark shows where you are.

Sources

Title card
The opening card is Gill Sans, slightly condensed, with a capital O where a zero would be. Gill Sans Light was modified for that card. Later cards, including Jupiter Mission and Jupiter and Beyond the Infinite, are Futura, with a few letters altered. Typeset in the Future. Fonts In Use. The title here asks for Gill Sans, then Futura. The text asks for Helvetica. No font file is loaded.
White
Discovery is stark white, accented with red and burnt orange. The room at the end is a stark white classical room with a bright floor. Tony Masters, Harry Lange, and Ernest Archer led the production design. Art Directors Guild. The page ground, #f4f4f1, is a working stand-in for that painted white, not a color taken from a frame.
HAL red
The eye is a Nikkor 8mm f/8 fisheye. An R60 red filter sits at the rear, and a tungsten bulb shines through it. The bulb overpowers the filter at the center, so the core is yellow-white and the rim is red. Kosmo Foto. The Age of Plastic. The lamp follows that core and rim. The mark, the focus ring, and the hover underline use #c41218, the same red held dark enough to read on white.
Ten principles
In the late 1970s Dieter Rams asked whether his own design was good. Vitsœ states the ten principles as: good design is innovative; makes a product useful; is aesthetic; makes a product understandable; is unobtrusive; is honest; is long-lasting; is thorough down to the last detail; is environmentally-friendly; and is as little design as possible. Vitsœ.
Braun
The SK 4, by Hans Gugelot and Dieter Rams, is a white painted metal body with elm sides and a clear lid. Die Neue Sammlung lists white, colourless, and grey, and records the nickname Snow White's coffin. The grey band on this page is that control surface. Die Neue Sammlung. On the later SK 55, one transmitting button is white with a red dot. Design und Text. The 606 Universal Shelving System, 1960, is Rams's shelving for Vitsœ. Vitsœ.
