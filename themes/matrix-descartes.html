Matrix / Descartes

The choice

01 Appearance

The signs keep coming. Read them as the room, the body, and the hour.

02 Doubt

Set every sign aside.

Sample first principle

I am, I exist.

Ego sum, ego existo.

Sample paragraph

The rain at the edge of the screen is a world made of signs. They fall in order, they can be read, and they can still be set aside. Doubt is the matching half of the frame: the same width, the same border, and almost nothing kept inside it. The clearing leaves room for one sentence, held still long enough to be checked again. Each time it is thought, it is either certain or it is not.
